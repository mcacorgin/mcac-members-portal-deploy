# Interim Privacy Disclosure for Membership Applications

MCAC's final privacy notice and member undertaking are still being finalized. This interim disclosure applies while you submit a membership application.

MCAC collects the identity and contact information supplied through LinkedIn, the professional details and expertise you enter, your contact-visibility choices, and any application material you upload. MCAC uses this information to review your application, communicate with you about it, prevent misuse, and operate the members portal.

Your application remains pending until an MCAC administrator explicitly approves it. Pending applicants do not receive member access, and their application details are not shown in the member directory. Authorized MCAC administrators and the service providers needed to operate the portal may process the information for these purposes.

Application information is retained while MCAC reviews and administers membership. Portal attachment files are automatically deleted after 60 days unless an administrator records a retention exception; the related post or application record may remain for continuity and audit purposes.

To ask about your information or request correction or deletion, contact admin@mcac.org.in.

By continuing, you acknowledge this interim disclosure. MCAC may ask you to review and accept the final privacy notice and member undertaking when they are published.
