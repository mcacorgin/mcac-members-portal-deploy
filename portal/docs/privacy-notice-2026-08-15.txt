MCAC Privacy Notice and Consent

Effective Date: 15/08/2026
Last Updated: 15/08/2026

1. Who We Are

MCAC – Marathi Corporate Advisory Collective ("MCAC", "we", "us" or "our") is a professional community and network intended to facilitate professional networking, knowledge sharing, collaboration, business opportunities and community engagement among its members.

For the purposes of applicable data protection law, MCAC will act as the Data Fiduciary in relation to the personal data collected through our registration and membership processes.

2. Personal Data We Collect

When you register with MCAC using your LinkedIn profile or otherwise provide information to us, we may collect and process the following categories of personal data:

Your name;

LinkedIn profile URL and publicly available professional profile information that you choose to share with us;

Professional details such as your current organisation, designation, industry, professional experience and areas of expertise;

Profile photograph, where made available or voluntarily provided;

Email address and other contact details voluntarily provided by you;

City or general location, where voluntarily provided;

Professional interests, skills and areas in which you may wish to collaborate;

Information submitted by you through membership forms, surveys, events or other MCAC activities; and

Communications or interactions you voluntarily have with MCAC.

MCAC will only collect personal data that is reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this notice.

3. Why We Use Your Personal Data

Your personal data may be processed for the following specified purposes:

To verify, review and manage your membership or registration with MCAC;

To create and maintain your member profile and community directory, where applicable;

To communicate with you regarding MCAC activities, meetings, events, initiatives and opportunities;

To facilitate professional networking and collaboration among MCAC members;

To identify members with relevant expertise, interests or professional capabilities for specific discussions, projects or opportunities;

To administer surveys, feedback initiatives and community programmes;

To maintain the security and integrity of MCAC's digital platforms and member records;

To respond to your requests, queries or grievances; and

To comply with applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

MCAC will not use your personal data for purposes materially unrelated to those described above without providing an appropriate notice and obtaining consent where required.

4. LinkedIn-Based Registration

If you choose to register or identify yourself using your LinkedIn profile, MCAC may collect the information that you voluntarily provide or authorise to be shared with us as part of the registration process.

MCAC does not require your LinkedIn password.

Where you provide only your LinkedIn profile URL, MCAC may review information that is publicly accessible through that profile for the purpose of understanding your professional background and determining the relevance of your membership to the MCAC community.

Your use of LinkedIn and the processing of data by LinkedIn remain subject to LinkedIn's own terms and privacy policies.

5. How We May Share Your Information

MCAC may share relevant professional information with:

Other MCAC members, where this is necessary to facilitate networking, collaboration or community engagement and where such sharing is consistent with the purpose communicated to you;

Service providers or technology partners who support our membership management, communications, events or digital infrastructure, subject to appropriate contractual or organisational safeguards;

Organisers or partners involved in an MCAC programme, event or initiative where your participation requires such sharing; and

Government authorities, regulators or other parties where disclosure is required by applicable law.

MCAC does not sell personal data as a commercial product.

6. Visibility of Your Professional Profile

MCAC may create a member directory or professional network that includes selected information such as your:

Name;

Organisation;

Professional designation;

Area of expertise;

LinkedIn profile link; and

Other professional information that you have expressly agreed to make available to the MCAC community.

Where MCAC intends to make your profile information visible to other members, the relevant scope of visibility will be communicated to you.

You may request correction, modification or removal of information from the member directory, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

7. Data Retention

MCAC will retain your personal data only for as long as it is necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including:

Maintaining your membership or relationship with MCAC;

Facilitating participation in MCAC activities;

Maintaining appropriate business and administrative records;

Addressing disputes or grievances; or

Meeting applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

When your personal data is no longer required for these purposes, MCAC will take reasonable steps to delete, erase or otherwise dispose of it in accordance with applicable law and our data retention practices.

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request to:

Obtain information about the personal data being processed by MCAC;

Request correction or completion of inaccurate or incomplete personal data;

Request erasure of personal data where applicable;

Withdraw consent for future processing of personal data where processing is based on your consent; and

Raise a grievance regarding the processing of your personal data.

Withdrawal of consent will not affect the lawfulness of processing undertaken before such withdrawal. However, withdrawal of consent may affect MCAC's ability to provide certain membership, networking or community services to you.

To exercise your rights or submit a request, please contact us at:

9. Data Security

MCAC will take reasonable organisational and technical measures to protect personal data under its control from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss, misuse or destruction.

Access to personal data will be limited to persons or service providers who reasonably require such access for authorised purposes.

While we take reasonable steps to protect personal data, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Withdrawal of Consent

You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting MCAC at the details provided above.

Where feasible, MCAC will provide a mechanism for you to withdraw consent that is reasonably accessible and comparable to the mechanism through which consent was provided.

Following withdrawal, MCAC may cease processing the relevant personal data unless retention or processing is otherwise permitted or required under applicable law.

11. Grievance Redressal

If you have a concern, complaint or grievance regarding the processing of your personal data, you may contact:

Grievance Officer / Privacy Contact: Sachin Kelkar, Cofounder
Email: sachinke@gmail.com

MCAC will address grievances in accordance with applicable law and its internal grievance-handling procedures.

Where applicable law permits, you may also have the right to approach the appropriate statutory authority after following the applicable grievance redressal process.

12. Changes to This Privacy Notice

MCAC may update this Privacy Notice from time to time to reflect changes in our activities, data processing practices or applicable legal requirements.

The updated version will be made available through the MCAC registration platform, website or other appropriate communication channel.
